Tiny Friends

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Ambitious Seed develops Tiny Friends. This policy explains how Ambitious Seed ("we", "us") handles information when you use Tiny Friends, also listed as My Tiny Friends on some stores. Features and service providers can differ by platform, version, region, and your choices.

Game information and local storage

The game stores your player name, pet names, pets, inventory, in-game currency, achievements, purchase-related entitlements, preferences, and other progress on your device. Names entered in the game do not need to be your real name. You do not need to provide an email address to create a local game profile.

Some information is also transmitted off your device for cloud saving, analytics, advertising, purchase processing, and diagnostics, as described below. A local game profile does not mean that all information stays on your device.

Google Play Games and cloud saves

On supported Android versions, Google Play Games Services provides platform sign-in, achievements, and cloud saves. These features use your Google Play Games player identifier and authentication status. Cloud saves contain your game progress, including player and pet names, inventory, achievements, and purchase-related game benefits.

You can control automatic Google Play Games connection and cloud synchronization through the available game settings. Disabling synchronization does not delete an existing cloud save or stop the separate analytics and diagnostic processing described in this policy. A cloud restore may bring previously saved progress back to your device.

Advertising, analytics, and attribution

Tiny Friends includes advertising and analytics services. Depending on the platform and enabled services, they process advertising identifiers, installation and other device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device model, operating system, app version, language, network information, app launches, gameplay events, ad impressions and clicks, and technical performance or diagnostic information.

We use this information to understand game usage, improve reliability, measure advertising campaigns and ad revenue, and detect invalid traffic. Advertising partners also use data to deliver and measure ads and, where permitted and subject to applicable choices, personalize advertising. Approximate location inferred from an IP address does not require a location permission. A device permission alone therefore does not control all location-related processing.

Consent or privacy choices may be presented by advertising services where applicable. You can also use your device's advertising identifier and tracking controls. These choices may limit personalized advertising or identifier access; they do not necessarily stop all advertising, measurement, or technical data collection.

Purchases

Google Play or Apple's App Store processes payments made through the relevant store. We do not receive your full payment-card number or bank account details from these purchases.

The game and its service providers process product identifiers, transaction or order identifiers, purchase status, purchase tokens or receipts and signatures where applicable, price, currency, and purchase time to deliver game items, validate purchases, investigate payment problems, prevent fraud, and measure revenue. Relevant purchase information is also included in analytics and diagnostic reports.

Diagnostics and security

The app automatically sends technical reports to identify errors, restore functionality, and investigate suspected abuse. Reports can include the app version, error details, operation status, player and pet names, elapsed play time, and game-progress or purchase information relevant to the event. Troubleshooting tools can also transmit more detailed game-state information when used to investigate a problem.

Third-party cloud messaging services are used to deliver and store technical notifications. Access to our diagnostic reports is limited to authorized people handling technical support and application security. Advertising and analytics SDKs may separately collect technical diagnostics under their own privacy practices. A suspicious-activity report is a diagnostic signal and does not by itself establish that a player has committed fraud.

Service providers and recipients

Depending on your platform and the services enabled in your version, information is handled through the following providers. Some process information on our behalf; advertising and platform providers may also process information for their own purposes under their privacy policies.

Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Firebase Analytics, and Google AdMob provide platform features, purchases, analytics, and advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Apple and the App Store provide distribution and purchase processing on iOS. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Appodeal and its mediation partners provide advertising, ad measurement, and related services. Integrated partners include Google AdMob / Ad Manager, Amazon Publisher Services, AppLovin, BidMachine, Bidon, Bigo Ads, Chartboost, DT Exchange, InMobi, ironSource / Unity LevelPlay, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Moloco, myTarget / VK Ads, Smaato, Unity Ads, Liftoff Monetize / Vungle, and Yandex. Not every partner serves ads in every session. Meta and Sentry integrations may also provide analytics or diagnostics. See Appodeal's Privacy Policy and its data disclosure documentation; individual partners have their own privacy policies.

AppsFlyer and Tenjin provide attribution, campaign and revenue measurement, analytics, and fraud-related signals. See the AppsFlyer and Tenjin privacy policies.

Supabase hosts our analytics backend, which receives installation identifiers, attribution profiles, and advertising-revenue and purchase events. See Supabase's privacy information and Data Processing Addendum.

Telegram provides delivery and storage of technical notifications. See Telegram's Privacy Policy. Our email provider also processes support correspondence.

We may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. The providers used depend on the platform, app version, and enabled features.

Contacting support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and information you choose to include, such as a description of a problem, screenshots, transaction references, or diagnostic logs. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, and provide support. Please do not send passwords, full card details, or unrelated sensitive information.

Purposes and legal bases

We process information to provide game features and purchases, maintain and restore progress, diagnose problems, answer support requests, measure usage and revenue, deliver and measure advertising, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, processing necessary to provide a feature or fulfill a purchase may be based on performance of a contract; proportionate reliability, support, and security processing may be based on legitimate interests, subject to your rights and interests. Consent is the applicable basis where required for advertising, tracking, or related processing, and legal obligations may require certain records to be retained. The basis depends on the purpose and circumstances; accepting this policy is not a substitute for any separately required consent.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until it is removed through available game or device controls. Removing local data or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete cloud saves, store transaction records, analytics records, or technical reports already transmitted.

For information under our control, retention is determined by its purpose: cloud progress supports ongoing play and restoration; technical reports and support correspondence support investigation and follow-up; purchase and security records support delivery, fraud investigation, dispute resolution, and applicable legal obligations. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for these purposes. Providers may apply separate retention periods to information they control, as described in their policies.

You can request access to or deletion of information under our control at ambitiousseedgame@gmail.com. Include the game name and enough information to locate the relevant records, such as your in-game name, approximate event date, or transaction reference. Do not send passwords. We may need to verify your request. We will explain any applicable retention requirement or limitation. Google Play Games also provides controls for deleting game data associated with your Google account; store purchase records are managed by the relevant platform.

Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to a data protection authority. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place before it.

Use available in-game cloud settings, advertising consent controls, device tracking settings, and platform privacy tools for the features they cover. Contact us for requests concerning information under our control. Switching off one feature does not necessarily stop processing by other features or remove data already stored.

International processing and security

Our providers operate internationally, so information may be processed outside your country, including in countries with different data protection rules. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, the relevant transfer must be covered by an applicable legal mechanism. You can contact us for information about transfers relating to your data.

We use reasonable measures to protect information and limit access. Our direct requests to the analytics backend and technical-notification service use HTTPS. Cloud messaging is not the same as end-to-end encrypted storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's privacy

Tiny Friends is not directed to children under 13. Parents and guardians should review the game's store age and audience information before allowing a child to use it. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required.

Changes and scope

This policy covers Tiny Friends, including versions listed as My Tiny Friends. Tiny Friends Farm has a separate privacy policy. Browsing our website, using a distribution platform, or visiting external links may involve separate privacy practices.

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change and communicate material changes as required by applicable law.
