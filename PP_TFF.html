Tiny Friends Farm

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Ambitious Seed develops Tiny Friends Farm. This policy explains how information is handled in the current game, including development and test builds.

Game data on your device

Tiny Friends Farm does not require an account. Your farm, pets, eggs, inventory, in-game currency, achievements, and other progress are saved locally on your device. Preferences such as language and screen orientation are also stored locally. The game does not upload these saves to Ambitious Seed or provide its own cloud-save service.

The current game does not include advertising, in-app purchases, or a gameplay analytics service. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The game does not request your contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera.

Unity diagnostics

The game is built with Unity, and engine diagnostic data collection is enabled in the current project. Unity may process technical information about crashes, errors, device and software configuration, and an installation-specific identifier to help identify and resolve stability problems. This is separate from gameplay analytics and advertising.

Unity processes diagnostic data on our behalf. Its documented default retention period for personal diagnostic data is 90 days. See Unity's Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity's Diagnostics privacy overview.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as a description of a problem, screenshots, or diagnostic logs. We use that information to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information.

Support messages are handled through our email provider. We retain them only as needed to handle the request, follow up on the issue, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Purposes and sharing

We use information to operate the game, investigate technical problems, and answer support requests. Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on our legitimate interests in maintaining a stable game and responding to requests, subject to your rights and interests, or on consent where required.

Access is limited to people and service providers who need the information for these purposes. We may also disclose information when required by law. Providers may process information outside your country, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local progress remains on your device until you reset it or remove the app's data. Use Reset Progress in the game settings to start a new farm; this keeps language and orientation preferences. To remove all locally stored game data and preferences, use your device's app-data controls or remove the app and its data. Device or operating-system backups may retain a separate copy under your backup settings.

Resetting local progress does not delete support correspondence or diagnostic data already sent to Unity. Contact us to request access to or deletion of that information. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to correct information, restrict or object to processing, receive a portable copy, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, and complain to your local data protection authority. We may need information to verify and locate your request.

Children's privacy

The game does not ask players to provide a name, email address, or other contact information. Diagnostic processing is described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and arrange deletion where appropriate.

Security and external services

We take reasonable measures to protect information we handle, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Your device, operating system, distribution platform, and any external websites you choose to visit have their own privacy practices. This policy covers Tiny Friends Farm, rather than browsing the Ambitious Seed website.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when our practices change. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law. New data-collecting features will be reflected in this policy before they are introduced.
